These are not a magazine. There are three of them because there are three questions the calculators keep raising and the result panel is too small to answer. One walks the SSS salary-credit steps the contribution and net-pay tools use. One says what the MPF line on a payslip is, and what it is not, next to Pag-IBIG. One shows how a diminishing-balance payment splits into interest and principal, which is the schedule behind the loan calculators.

The peso figures in each guide are produced by the same functions as the forms. If a rate in Rates & sources changes, the guide’s examples have to change with it. We are not adding parallel articles that restate the same deduction in a new city or a new year.
