How SSS loans are computed

An SSS salary loan is based on the average of your latest 12 monthly salary credits. A one-month loan is about one average MSC and a two-month loan about two, subject to SSS limits. SSS deducts a 1% service fee and the interest due until your first payment, so the cash you receive is lower than the loan amount. The balance is repaid in 24 monthly payments on a diminishing balance.

The formula

You receive = loan amount − 1% service fee − advance interest
Monthly payment = standard loan payment formula over 24 months at the annual rate

Salary loan or emergency loan?

The emergency loan is a separate program with its own eligibility, a lower interest rate, no service fee and a payment moratorium before repayment starts. Use the loan type switch above to compare them.

Rates, limits and eligibility are set by SSS circulars and can change. Treat this as a planning estimate and verify it on the SSS website or My.SSS.
