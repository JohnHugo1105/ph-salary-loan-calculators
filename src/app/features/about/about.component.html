Our mission

PhCalculators.com is a free tool built by Filipino developers to help employees, freelancers, and business owners in the Philippines compute their net pay, government contributions (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG), and loan amortizations.

PH Calculators is built and run by Take IT Thru Solutions, the public brand of TakeThru Software Development Services, a business registered in the Philippines.

We focus on clarity, speed, and transparency. Unlike tools that hide the logic, we show the formulas and assumptions behind every result, so you can check the numbers against your payslip.

Accuracy and sources

Our calculators use public guidance and official tables from the BIR, SSS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG circulars. Every rate and limit is listed, with its effective date and source, on the Rates & sources page.

Actual employer policies or official tables may differ slightly. Always verify with official government sources or your HR department.

Privacy and terms

We respect your privacy. All calculations are performed on your device, and we do not store or sell your personal salary data. Read the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service and Disclaimer.
