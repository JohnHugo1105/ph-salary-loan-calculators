How it is computed

Income needed assumes the monthly amortization should not exceed 35% of your gross monthly income.

Rates change. After the repricing period ends, Pag-IBIG resets the rate and your payment can go up or down. The rates listed here are the ones this calculator uses, so confirm the current schedule with Pag-IBIG before you apply.

One rate, held flat, on a diminishing balance

The payment above is principal and interest only. Each month the interest is the remaining balance times the monthly rate, and the rest of the payment reduces the balance. The monthly rate is the annual rate divided by 12. That is the whole of computeAmortization, which this page shares with the loan amortization calculator. Nothing in the function adds mortgage redemption insurance, fire insurance, or a processing fee. Pag-IBIG’s own statement will be higher once those are on it.

The rate is fixed only for the repricing period in the dropdown. This calculator then pretends that same rate lasts until the last payment, because that is the only way to show a single schedule. When the fixing period ends, Pag-IBIG sets a new rate for the next period and the payment changes. A 3-year fixing period on a 20-year loan is not a 20-year fixed rate. Budget for the payment to move, in either direction, at the reset.

The rates in the dropdown are the ones stored for this calculator: 1 year at {{ rates[1] }}%, 3 years at {{ rates[3] }}%, 5 at {{ rates[5] }}%, 10 at {{ rates[10] }}%, 15 at {{ rates[15] }}%, 20 at {{ rates[20] }}%, 25 at {{ rates[25] }}%, and 30 at {{ rates[30] }}%. Three schedules from those stored rates

₱1,000,000 for 20 years, 3-year rate of {{ exA.rate }}%

The payment is ₱{{ exA.payment | number: '1.2-2' }} a month for {{ exA.n }} months, if {{ exA.rate }}% never changes. The first month’s interest is ₱{{ exA.first.interest | number: '1.2-2' }} and only ₱{{ exA.first.principal | number: '1.2-2' }} comes off the principal. The last month flips that: interest ₱{{ exA.last.interest | number: '1.2-2' }}, principal ₱{{ exA.last.principal | number: '1.2-2' }}. Payments over the full term are ₱{{ exA.totalPaid | number: '1.2-2' }}, so interest is ₱{{ exA.totalInterest | number: '1.2-2' }} on top of the ₱1,000,000. Dividing the payment by 0.35 gives a gross monthly income of ₱{{ exA.requiredIncome | number: '1.2-2' }}. That 35% test is this site’s rule of thumb. It ignores insurance and it is not a capacity letter from Pag-IBIG. Holding {{ exA.rate }}% for all 20 years is a picture of that one rate, not a forecast of the reset.

₱2,000,000 for 30 years, 5-year rate of {{ exB.rate }}%

Payment ₱{{ exB.payment | number: '1.2-2' }}. First month: interest ₱{{ exB.first.interest | number: '1.2-2' }}, principal ₱{{ exB.first.principal | number: '1.2-2' }}. Across {{ exB.n }} payments the interest is ₱{{ exB.totalInterest | number: '1.2-2' }}, more than the amount borrowed, which is what a 30-year diminishing-balance loan does at this rate. The 35% income line is ₱{{ exB.requiredIncome | number: '1.2-2' }}. The calculator will accept a loan up to ₱{{ rules.maxLoan | number: '1.0-0' }}. It will not tell you whether Pag-IBIG will lend ₱2,000,000 against a particular property. Appraisal, loan-to-value, and the member’s age at the end of the term are Pag-IBIG’s tests, and they are not in this function. Confirm them with the agency.

₱500,000 for 15 years, 1-year rate of {{ exC.rate }}%

Payment ₱{{ exC.payment | number: '1.2-2' }}. Interest over the term ₱{{ exC.totalInterest | number: '1.2-2' }}. Income line ₱{{ exC.requiredIncome | number: '1.2-2' }}. The first month still puts ₱{{ exC.first.interest | number: '1.2-2' }} into interest and ₱{{ exC.first.principal | number: '1.2-2' }} into principal. A one-year fixing period means the stored rate is the one you should trust for the shortest time. Twelve months after release, the payment on this page and the payment on the statement can already be different numbers.

A promotional rate this calculator does not use

Philstar reported on 21 June 2026 that applications received until 31 December 2026 would be priced at 4.5% for three years above the socialized-housing ceiling up to a low-cost ceiling the article put at ₱2.5 million, and at 5.75% for three years above that up to ₱10 million, then repriced (Philstar, 21 June 2026). Later write-ups give a different peso ceiling for the 4.5% band. We did not get a Pag-IBIG circular to settle it, so confirm the ceiling with the agency. This dropdown does not apply 4.5% and does not switch rates after year three.

Who this calculator cannot qualify

Posted savings, age at the end of the term, the property, and any co-borrower are decided on the application. This page has none of those inputs. A payment inside the 35% line can still be refused. The ₱{{ rules.maxLoan | number: '1.0-0' }} ceiling is only the largest amount the form lets you type.

How to check the figure in Virtual Pag-IBIG

  1. Sign in to Virtual Pag-IBIG from the Pag-IBIG Fund site and open the housing-loan calculator or the application, not the MP2 savings screen.
  2. Enter the same loan amount, term, and fixing period. Compare the principal-and-interest amortization only. If their rate is not the rate in our dropdown, their payment will not match, and their rate is the one that matters.
  3. Add back, in your own budget, the insurance line they show. Our total interest does not include it.
  4. If the application is under the 2026 promotional pricing, stop using the rate in our dropdown for the first three years. Ask Pag-IBIG which ceiling your loan falls under before you lock a fixing period.

Mistakes that make a housing estimate look cheaper than the loan

  • Reading a 3-year or 5-year rate as if it lasted the whole term. The examples do that on purpose, and they say so.
  • Comparing our payment with a quote that already includes insurance or a month of advance amortization. Those are extra cash.
  • Treating the income line as net pay, or stacking a second loan on the same 35%. Confirm which income Pag-IBIG counts.
  • Trusting the 1-year and 3-year stored rates. They are the ones we were least able to re-check. Confirm with the agency.
