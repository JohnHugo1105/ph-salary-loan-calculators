How it is computed

Income needed assumes the monthly amortization should not exceed 35% of your gross monthly income.

Rates change. After the repricing period ends, Pag-IBIG resets the rate and your payment can go up or down. The rates listed here are the ones this calculator uses, so confirm the current schedule with Pag-IBIG before you apply.

How the housing loan payment is worked out

A Pag-IBIG housing loan is repaid in equal monthly amortizations on a diminishing balance. The rate is fixed only for the repricing period you choose (for example 3 or 5 years). After that, Pag-IBIG resets the rate for the remaining term, so your payment can change. A longer repricing period gives you a steadier payment but usually a higher starting rate.

Why the income needed matters

Lenders check that your monthly amortization is affordable. This calculator shows the gross monthly income you would need if the payment should be no more than 35% of it. Your own approval also depends on your credit and other requirements, so treat it as a rough guide.

Not included: mortgage redemption insurance, fire insurance, processing and other fees. Compare any figure here with the computation Pag-IBIG gives you.
