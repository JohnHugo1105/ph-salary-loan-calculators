MPF/WISP is not an extra charge. It is the part of your SSS contribution that is based on salary credit above ₱{{ regularCap | number: '1.0-0' }}. It is shown separately because it goes to your provident savings.

The payslip column and the column you never see

Every row in the table above has two payers. The left column is withheld from you. The right column is an extra cost to the employer, or, if there is no employer, a second amount you remit yourself. The percentages, floors, and caps are listed once on Rates & sources. What this page is for is the split: who actually hands the money over.

MPF/WISP is not a fourth program you pay on top of SSS. It is the slice of the same 15% that sits on salary credit above ₱{{ regularCap | number: '1.0-0' }}. The MPF payslip note is the longer version of that distinction.

Self-employed and voluntary: you are both columns

There is no one to take the right-hand column. Self-employed and voluntary members pay the employee share and the employer share of the salary credit they declare to SSS. On the ₱{{ example.salary | number: '1.0-0' }} example, adding the columns is ₱{{ example.both | number: '1.2-2' }} a month if the EC premium in the employer column is part of what you remit. This calculator always files EC in the employer column and does not have a separate self-employed switch. Confirm with SSS whether your membership type pays that ₱10 or ₱30, and confirm the lowest credit you are allowed to declare. The brackets themselves do not change.

PhilHealth without an employer is the full premium, ₱{{ example.philhealthTotal | number: '1.2-2' }} at ₱{{ example.salary | number: '1.0-0' }}, not the ₱{{ example.philhealth | number: '1.2-2' }} an employee sees removed from a payslip. Pag-IBIG is the same 2% plus 2%, capped, unless you are under the ₱1,500 salary where the member rate drops to 1% and the employer rate stays at 2%. A voluntary Pag-IBIG savings amount above the mandatory ₱200 is a choice. It is not what this mandatory calculator adds. The separate MP2 calculator is for the five-year savings account, which is not the mandatory contribution.
