What happens when you write

Mail to that address is read by the person who maintains the calculators. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. A business day here means a weekday that is not a Philippine public holiday. If we need the circular you are citing, the reply will say so instead of guessing a new rate.

For a wrong result, include the page URL, the inputs you typed, the figure the page showed, and the figure you expected. A screenshot of the breakdown helps. If you are comparing with an agency, name the circular, the press release, or the disclosure statement, and quote the part that disagrees. Every rate we use is listed on Rates & sources.

What we can and cannot do

We can correct a rate in the calculator when you show where it is published, and we can explain which function produced a line. We cannot file an SSS, Pag-IBIG, PhilHealth, or BIR transaction, tell you whether a loan will be approved, or compute the award an agency will release. Those answers stay with the agency and, for payroll, with your employer.

There is no contact form and no street address on this page. We have not published a personal biography or a business registration number. The operator named on the About page is the publisher.

Please do not send government ID numbers, full contribution printouts, or bank account numbers. The inputs in your message are enough.
