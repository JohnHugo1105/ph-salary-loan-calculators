PH Calculators does not ask you to create an account, and it does not sell personal information. The calculators run in your browser. What leaves your device is described below, and it is narrower than a typical “analytics and advertising” policy because this site’s code does not include a separate analytics product.

What the calculators keep on your device

Salary figures, loan amounts, and results are processed in the browser. Saving a calculation writes it to this browser’s local storage under a key this site uses. We do not receive that save, and clearing site data removes it. A share link encodes the inputs in the URL so someone else can open the same estimate. Following that link sends the URL to whatever host delivers the page. It is not stored in a database of ours.

Your theme (light, dark, or system) and whether you dismissed the cookie notice are also in local storage, so the next visit can remember them.

Advertising

The site is supported by Google AdSense automatic ads. The only advertising script in this project is the AdSense loader. It is not placed on the saved-calculations page, and the standalone “page not found” file does not include it. We do not add other ad networks, and we do not paste manual ad units into the articles.

Google uses cookies to serve and measure ads to visitors of this site, based on visits here and on other sites that use Google’s services. That is a description of what can happen when you browse phcalculators.com. It is not a publisher’s instruction about “your users” or “your sites”.

Google explains this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can control personalized ads in Google’s Ads Settings.

Analytics

As of {{ updated }}, the site’s code does not load Google Analytics, Matomo, Plausible, or any other analytics tag. The usage we can see is what the host and Google’s ad systems record in the course of delivering the page and the ads. We do not run a first-party analytics account against your calculator inputs, because those inputs are not sent to us as form data.

Email you send us

If you write to the address on the contact page, we use the message to answer it and, when you are reporting a rate, to compare it with the circular you cite. We do not add that address to a mailing list. The message sits in the mailbox that receives it.

The Data Privacy Act

Republic Act No. 10173, the Data Privacy Act of 2012, is the Philippine law on personal information. This site is operated from the Philippines, as stated on the About page. We do not ask for your name, government number, or salary in order to use a calculator. This page does not publish a data-protection officer or a registration number, because those details are not established in the site. If you need them, ask the operator. Do not treat this policy as a registration certificate.
