How the SSS maternity benefit is computed

The Expanded Maternity Leave Law (RA 11210) gives qualified female workers 105 days of paid maternity leave for a live birth, 120 days for a solo parent, and 60 days for a miscarriage or emergency termination of pregnancy. SSS pays the daily maternity allowance, and your employer is generally required to pay any difference between that and your full salary.

The formula

Average daily salary credit = total of your six highest monthly salary credits ÷ 180
Maternity benefit = average daily salary credit × days of leave

For this benefit, SSS counts each monthly salary credit only up to ₱20,000, so the largest six-month total is ₱120,000. That makes the largest regular benefit ₱70,000 for 105 days (₱80,000 for 120 days and ₱40,000 for 60 days).

Who qualifies

  • You have paid at least 3 monthly contributions in the 12 months before the semester of your delivery or miscarriage.
  • You notified your employer (or SSS, if you are self-employed or voluntary) of your pregnancy.

Rules and procedures change. Always confirm with SSS how your own contribution record and delivery date apply.
