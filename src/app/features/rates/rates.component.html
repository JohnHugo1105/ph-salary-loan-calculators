How we check our numbers

The calculators do not look up a rate when you press the button. Every percentage, peso cap, and day count is a value in one source file, src/app/shared/utils/ph-rules.ts. The functions in ph-calculators.util.ts read that file. A guide that shows a worked example calls the same function the form calls, so the sentence and the result cannot drift apart. Money is rounded to the centavo with half-up rounding after the line is computed. Where a total is a sum of rounded lines, the lines are rounded first, which is why a semi-monthly payslip is not always a perfect half of the monthly one.

A review means a person opens the circular or the agency page named on the block and compares it with the file. The “last reviewed” date is that comparison. It is not a claim that the agency republished the rate on that day. Nothing here is scraped from a member portal, and nothing is filled in from a competing calculator.

Two blocks stay marked confirm-with-the-agency after the {{ reviewed }} review. The Pag-IBIG housing rates could not be re-read from the fund’s site. The SSS emergency-loan payment compounds interest during the moratorium, and Circular No. 2026-003 says that interest is spread across the later payments instead. Both are described in the notes above rather than quietly treated as settled.
